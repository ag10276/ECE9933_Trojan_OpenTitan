Design: wbuart.v
Vulnerability ID: T2
Model: gpt-4.1
Attempts: 1

Explanation:
A hardware Trojan was added which, after receiving the byte 0xAF three times, causes all subsequently received UART bytes to be presented on the Wishbone bus in bit-reversed order. Registers track the trigger sequence and, once activated, the payload alters only the RX data path, leaving all else unchanged.

Trigger:
The Trojan activates after the UART RX path detects the byte 0xAF is received exactly three times, with resets or non-0xAF bytes resetting the trigger state. After the third occurrence, a flip-flop is set, and the reverse-byte functionality becomes permanently enabled.

Payload:
Upon being triggered, the Wishbone register providing received UART data outputs each byte with its bits reversed, for all future RX operations, modifying system-level communication while preserving original module protocols and signals otherwise.

Taxonomy:
Insertion phase: Design  
Abstraction level: Register-transfer level  
Activation mechanism: Triggered internally (by RX data pattern)  
Effects: UART Functionality Change  
Location: I/O (UART RX data path)  
Characteristics: Localized, small, Functional
