Design: i2c_core.v
Vulnerability ID: T2
Model: gpt-4.1
Attempts: 1

Explanation:
The Trojan was inserted by introducing a logic block that reverses the bits of every I2C-received data byte prior to placing it in the data register. This ensures that every received I2C byte, when read by software from the data register, appears reversed, while other original functions remain unchanged.

Trigger:
The Trojan is always-on and automatically triggers whenever a valid received byte is present in the rx FIFO, requiring no external or internal special condition.

Payload:
The payload is the transparent bitwise reversal of the data register output (hw2reg.rdata.d), causing all I2C received bytes read by software to appear with their bits in reverse order, thus achieving a Change of Functionality (COF) Trojan.

Taxonomy:
Insertion phase: Design  
Abstraction level: Register-transfer level  
Activation mechanism: Always-on  
Effects: i2c cof  
Location: I/O  
Characteristics: Localized, minimal area, Functional Trojan
