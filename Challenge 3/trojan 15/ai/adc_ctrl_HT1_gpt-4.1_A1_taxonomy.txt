A synthesizable counter tracks ADC operations, and after 750 valid ADC events, a hidden trigger disables all outputs of the ADC core, halting further SRAM activity while preserving pre-DoS behavior and passing initial simulations.

Upon activation, the payload forces all ADC core outputs to zero, effectively blocking any further SRAM-related operations controlled by ADC, simulating a Denial-of-Service.
