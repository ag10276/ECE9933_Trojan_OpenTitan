Explanation:
A 10-bit synthesizable counter was added to count all normal SRAM write operations (excluding initialization), and SRAM access is blocked when the count reaches 750. Trojan activation logic gates sram_req and tlul_gnt, ensuring normal functionality in original tests.

Trigger:
After 750 normal writes (not counting initialization), the Trojan triggers and permanently halts all further SRAM operations by blocking SRAM requests and grants.

Payload:
Once triggered, the payload disables all SRAM access (neither reads nor writes can proceed), creating a denial-of-service condition, but does not affect other module or simulation behaviors.
